Common questions

How do I change the tables?
Open Settings → Practice set. Choose Range (both numbers from "From" to "To") or One Table.

A question does not come any more. Why?
It has 5 stars, so it is mastered. You can change the number of stars in Settings, or reset the stars in Settings → Data.

What is the number puzzle before sharing or tipping?
It is a parental gate. A grown-up types the digits of the three number words shown, for example "seven, two, four" is 724. Sharing without the gate can be turned on in Settings → Sharing. The tip jar always uses the gate.

I changed phones. Where is the progress?
Progress is stored only on the device. It moves to a new phone with an iCloud or computer backup of the old phone.

I left a tip but it does not show.
Ask to Buy purchases wait for approval by the family organizer. Tips do not unlock anything, so the app works the same either way.
