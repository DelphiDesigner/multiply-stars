Privacy Policy

Multiply Stars

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Short version: Multiply Stars does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app stores the following on your device only: the name and avatar entered on the welcome screens, the practice settings, the stars for each question, the number of wrong answers for each question, and a log of practice sessions (date, time, length and score). This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. It does not connect to any server owned by the developer.

Sharing

If you choose to share a badge or progress card, the app creates a picture that shows the name, avatar and scores, and opens the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. A grown-up is asked first. Nothing is shared automatically.

Purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. The developer does not receive your payment details. A grown-up is asked first.

Children

The app is made for children. Because no personal data is collected or sent anywhere, no personal data of children is collected by the developer.

Deleting data

Settings → Data → Erase all data removes everything from the app. Deleting the app also removes all its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
